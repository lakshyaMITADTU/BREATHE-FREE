What Happens When You Quit

Your Body's Remarkable
Recovery

Within minutes of your last cigarette, healing begins. Here's the science.

20 minutes
Heart Rate Drops

Your heart rate and blood pressure begin to fall. Circulation improves almost instantly.

12 hours
Carbon Monoxide Clears

CO levels drop to normal. Your blood can now carry oxygen far more efficiently.

2 weeks – 3 months
Lungs & Circulation Improve

Lung function increases up to 30%. Walking feels easier. Coughing decreases.

1 – 9 months
Cilia Grow Back

Tiny hair-like structures in your lungs regrow, clearing mucus and reducing infection.

1 year
Heart Disease Risk Halved

Your risk of coronary heart disease is half that of a smoker. The distance grows every day.

10 years
Cancer Risk Near Normal

Lung cancer risk falls to about half that of a smoker. Other cancers also decrease.
